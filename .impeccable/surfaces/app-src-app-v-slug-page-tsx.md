# Prospect viewer /v/[slug]

Mode: Read (with one Operate moment: the decision). Visitor: the seller's prospect, often on a phone, opening a quote from an email. Job: read the proposal comfortably, then sign, ask for a change, book a meeting, or ask a question. Constraints: the prospect never sees heat, scores or tracking data ("suivre sans fliquer"); privacy notice stays visible; works for PDF, web page and video; email gate and expiry gate stay minimal.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The document leads; Clozer is the quiet report sheet it is bound into. Refuses the SaaS doc-viewer with floating pills, gradients and chat bubbles competing with the proposal.
OWN-WORLD: Same thermography report world in its coldest register: cool white paper / graphite night, hairline frame, near-square corners, Archivo condensed only for the document title, sans for everything else. No ironbow anywhere on this surface: heat belongs to the seller.
STORY: I open the quote → I read it without friction → the decision bar is always one tap away → I act.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Thin header strip (seller's company/document title left, page count + discreet "Lien sécurisé Clozer" right), the document full width beneath, a bottom decision strip as a report form footer: "Valider & signer" primary ink button, "Demander un ajustement" and "Prendre RDV" as outline cells, separated by hairlines.
FORM: Rapport de thermographie (inherited world), seed: none (unavailable)
Signature interaction: the decision strip slides up once the reader reaches the pricing or last page; before that it is a compact single row.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
