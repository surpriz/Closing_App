# Landing clozer.club

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: anyone who sent something (a quote, a deck, a resume, a cold email sequence) and hears nothing back: solo sellers, SDRs, SME sales teams and their manager, marketers, job seekers. Action: create an account (Google one-click or email) and reach a first live link. Proof: the mechanism shown on labelled example data (the example quote reading, a video viewing, a campaign timeline) plus real app screens; no customers or numbers exist. Constraints: paid plans live (Free / Solo / Team / Enterprise, plus the connected-mailbox add-on for campaigns, prices on /tarifs), French and English (`/fr`, `/en`). Sending (one by one from Gmail/Outlook, pasted anywhere, or in a campaign from the seller's own mailbox) is a way in; the product is what happens after the send.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The quote seen through a thermal camera: the visitor sees where it heats up. Refuses the split hero with a screenshot and a row of feature cards.
OWN-WORLD: Building-thermography report. Cool white report paper, graphite ink, hairline report grid and numbered fields. Ironbow scale (deep indigo → violet → red-orange → yellow → white-hot) appears only on heat data: page plates, spot reticles, the calibrated scale bar. One texture: diagonal hatch for overdue follow-ups. Live counts in fixed tabular digits.
STORY: I sent it and hear nothing → Clozer shows who reads, which pages, who else → Clozer writes the follow-up and sends it at the right time (in a campaign, the sequence pauses the moment the prospect reads the pricing) → I sign up in one field.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Report header strip (logo, five nav cells: how it works, features, campaigns, use cases, pricing; login; "Créer un lien"). Left, the condensed headline "Sachez qui lit. Relancez au bon moment." with the heat words underlined by the scale, the lead naming quote / deck / resume / sequence and the three ways to send, then the "Client" field block = working signup (email + Google) with the reassurance row (14-day Team trial, no card, Europe, sign-in link). Right, the example proposal as four thermal plates heating live, reticle on p.4 Tarifs, horizontal scale and the reading summary under it.
PAGE ORDER: hero → the silence (attachment vs link table) → three steps (the send step lists Gmail/Outlook, chat apps, campaigns) → what you see (document, video pitch, team view) → Clozer follows up (Today board) → campaigns (sequence timeline figure, add-on price, Smart Link line) → the prospect's side → tracking without snooping (dark band) → the four plans in one strip → FAQ → closing signup. Folded sections keep their old ids as aliases.
FORM: Rapport de thermographie, candidate 7 of 7, seed: none (unavailable)
Signature interaction: plates heat over ~4 s as a simulated reading plays; reticle snaps to the hottest page; reduced motion shows the end state.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
