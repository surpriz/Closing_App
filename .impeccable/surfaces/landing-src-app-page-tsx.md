# Landing clozer.club

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: seller (indé, agence, PME, grand compte) who sends quotes and gets silence. Action: create an account (Google one-click or email) and reach a first live link. Proof: the mechanism shown on a labelled example quote; no customers, numbers or prices exist. Constraints: free during beta, segments (Solo / Équipe / Entreprise) without prices, French only.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The quote seen through a thermal camera: the visitor sees where it heats up. Refuses the split hero with a screenshot and a row of feature cards.
OWN-WORLD: Building-thermography report. Cool white report paper, graphite ink, hairline report grid and numbered fields. Ironbow scale (deep indigo → violet → red-orange → yellow → white-hot) appears only on heat data: page plates, spot reticles, the calibrated scale bar. One texture: diagonal hatch for overdue follow-ups. Live counts in fixed tabular digits.
STORY: My quote goes silent → Clozer shows which pages were read and for how long → I know who to follow up today → I sign up in one field.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Report header strip (logo, report n° "exemple", nav). Huge condensed headline left "Voyez où votre devis chauffe." with the heat word in the scale; right the report "Client" field block = working signup (email + Google). Below, full width, the six pages of the example quote as thermal plates heating live, Sp1 reticle on p.4 Tarifs, vertical scale bar at the right end. Footer strip: prospect line, hatch overdue chip, "Gratuit pendant la bêta".
FORM: Rapport de thermographie, candidate 7 of 7, seed: none (unavailable)
Signature interaction: plates heat over ~4 s as a simulated reading plays; reticle snaps to the hottest page; reduced motion shows the end state.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
